# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release. `Fload.Attribution` for .NET MAUI and .NET for iOS and
  Android (net9.0 and net10.0), over fload-attribution-ios 1.0.0 and
  fload-attribution-android 1.0.1. `Configure`, `SetUserId`,
  `InstallReference`. Every other platform builds and does nothing.
- The HTTP path for classic Xamarin apps (`snippets/http-path`,
  `docs/xamarin-http.md`).
